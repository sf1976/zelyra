# Zelyra 0.4.0 human acceptance decision

**Decision:** deferred to 0.5.0
**Decision authority:** project owner, at the owner's explicit direction on 2026-10-09
**Status:** independent human acceptance was not conducted for 0.4.0. This is not a test result, user-acceptance evidence, or a claim that the onboarding protocol passed.

The project owner directed that 0.4.0 proceed without the independent human study and that the study be mandatory for 0.5.0. The 0.4.0 release plan records this scope decision. The study protocol remains available at [0.4.0 onboarding study](0.4.0-onboarding-study.en.md) as a basis for future participant testing, but no 0.4.0 candidate was tested by an independent participant.

**0.5.0 requirement:** before publishing `v0.5.0`, an independent person who does not develop Zelyra must complete the onboarding and acceptance protocol against the exact published candidate. Record the candidate commit, date, redacted observations, blockers, and an explicit owner decision in a new 0.5.0 acceptance record. The 0.4.0 deferral does not satisfy this gate.
