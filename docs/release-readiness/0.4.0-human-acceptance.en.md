# Zelyra 0.4.0 human acceptance decision

**Decision:** deferred to 0.5.0
**Decision authority:** project owner, at the owner's explicit direction on 2026-10-09
**Status:** independent human acceptance was not conducted for 0.4.0. This is not a test result, user-acceptance evidence, or a claim that the onboarding protocol passed. The 0.5.0 study target below is historical and was superseded by the project owner's later decision to defer all independent studies to 1.0.0.

The project owner directed that 0.4.0 proceed without the independent human study and that the study be mandatory for 0.5.0. The 0.4.0 release plan records this scope decision. The study protocol remains available at [0.4.0 onboarding study](0.4.0-onboarding-study.en.md) as a basis for future participant testing, but no 0.4.0 candidate was tested by an independent participant.

**Superseding decision:** the independent study is deferred to 1.0.0. No 0.5.0 human study is required or claimed; the 0.5.0 technical release gates remain separate. The 1.0.0 plan requires independent participants to test the immutable published candidate before stable publication.
