# Zelyra Handbook

The handbook is available in synchronized editions and formats:

### 1. Complete Master Edition (Lehrbuch & Technische Referenz)
The comprehensive 75-chapter textbook, technical reference, and complete appendices:
- **[Deutsch: Vollständiges Zelyra-Handbuch (de/handbuch.md)](de/handbuch.md)**
- **[English: Complete Zelyra Handbook (en/handbook.md)](en/handbook.md)**
- **Online (Interactive with search):** [https://siedelmann.com/handbuch](https://siedelmann.com/handbuch) · [https://siedelmann.com/handbook](https://siedelmann.com/handbook)

### 2. Guided Introductory Edition
A concise, step-by-step introduction building a machine-management application:
- [Deutsch (Einstieg)](de/README.md)
- [English (Intro)](en/README.md)

Both editions are maintained as practical guides to the current Zelyra
implementation. The [implemented capabilities](../implemented.en.md) summarize
what is available, including its limits; the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) tracks
unfinished work. Release plans describe work in progress and are not evidence
that a planned feature is already available.

The [digital sovereignty manifesto](../MANIFESTO.md) is the binding product
compass; read it alongside the implementation status rather than as a claim of
completed guarantees.

For the binding order of language sources and the required validation workflow,
see the [source authority guide](../source-authority.md) and the
[deutsche Quellenlandkarte](../source-authority.de.md).

> Zelyra 1.0.5 is the current stable release. Supported workflows and limits
> are documented in the [implementation inventory](../implemented.en.md) and
> [database support matrix](../database-support.en.md). Keep backups, inspect
> generated SQL, and never test a destructive schema plan on data you cannot
> afford to lose.
